Color Contrast - Success Examples

All examples below pass WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.3 (minimum 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text / UI components).

Example 1: Black body text on white

This body text is #000000 on #FFFFFF (ratio 21:1). Maximum contrast, passes AAA.

Pass: #000 on #fff = 21:1 ≥ 7:1 (AAA)

Example 2: White text on black background

Important notice: offices are closed on Monday.

Pass: #fff on #000 = 21:1 ≥ 7:1 (AAA)

Example 3: Dark blue link text on white background

Read our terms and conditions for details.

Pass: #0b3d91 on #fff ≈ 10.2:1 ≥ 4.5:1, plus underline/ context distinguishes link

Example 4: White text on dark green background

Success: your payment was completed.

Pass: #fff on #1b5e20 ≈ 9.2:1 ≥ 4.5:1, does not rely on color alone

Example 5: Dark form label and input text


Pass: #1a1a1a on #fff ≈ 17.4:1 ≥ 4.5:1, visible label + strong border

Example 6: White text on dark-blue button

Pass: #fff on #0b3d91 ≈ 10.2:1 ≥ 4.5:1

Example 7: Dark gray secondary text on white

Product title with good contrast

$49.99 • In stock • Free shipping over $50

Pass: #424242 on #fff ≈ 10:1 ≥ 4.5:1

Example 8: Dark brown / maroon text on light yellow background

Limited time offer! Sale ends Friday at midnight.

Pass: #5d4037 on #fff8e1 ≈ 8.4:1 ≥ 4.5:1

Example 9: White text on dark red badge

NEW -20%
Pass: #fff on #b00020 ≈ 7.3:1 ≥ 4.5:1

Example 10: Dark green success message with icon and border

✓ Your profile was updated successfully!

Pass: #1b5e20 on #e6f4ea ≈ 8:1 ≥ 4.5:1, icon + border so not color-alone
