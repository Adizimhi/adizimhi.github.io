Color Contrast - Failure Examples

All examples below fail WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.3 (minimum 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text / UI components).

Example 1: Light gray body text on white

This body text is #999999 on #FFFFFF (ratio ~2.85:1). Fails 4.5:1 requirement.

Fail: #999 on #fff ≈ 2.85:1 < 4.5:1

Example 2: Yellow text on white background

Warning message in yellow on white is nearly invisible.

Fail: #FFFF00 on #fff ≈ 1.07:1 < 4.5:1

Example 3: Light blue link text on light gray background

Read our terms and conditions for details.

Fail: #7aa7d9 on #eeeeee ≈ 2.3:1 < 4.5:1

Example 4: Red text on green background

Error: payment failed, please try again.

Fail: #ff0000 on #00b300 ≈ 1.4:1 < 4.5:1, plus red/green color-blindness issue

Example 5: Low-contrast placeholder text in form field


Fail: #bbbbbb placeholder on #fff ≈ 1.9:1 < 4.5:1

Example 6: White text on light-blue button

Fail: #fff on #8ecae6 ≈ 1.7:1 < 4.5:1 (also fails 3:1 for UI)

Example 7: Light gray secondary / muted text

Product title with good contrast

$49.99 • In stock • Free shipping over $50

Fail: #c0c0c0 on #fff ≈ 1.6:1 < 4.5:1

Example 8: Orange text on white background

Limited time offer! Sale ends Friday at midnight.

Fail: #ffa500 on #fff ≈ 2.1:1 < 4.5:1

Example 9: White text on light pink badge

NEW -20%
Fail: #fff on #f4a7c3 ≈ 2.0:1 < 4.5:1

Example 10: Light green success message on white

✓ Your profile was updated successfully!

Fail: #90ee90 on #fff ≈ 1.4:1 < 4.5:1
